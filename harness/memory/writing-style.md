# Writing Style Profile

## General preferences
- Preserve the user's own natural, human voice. Avoid robotic or generic AI-sounding prose.
- Prefer direct, clear explanations and concrete details over inflated claims and marketing language.
- When asked only for grammar or typo correction, do not silently rewrite the substance.
- Keep technical writing accurate and practical; include exact commands and clearly distinguish what was implemented from what is only planned.

## Blog and LinkedIn
- Use a conversational, personal style.
- Avoid clichés, engagement bait, over-polished corporate announcements and generic AI introductions/conclusions.
- Preserve the user's original point of view and real experiences.
- Do not invent client details, results, numbers, quotes or achievements.

## Technical reports
- Make scope, methodology, evidence, impact, limitations and recommendations clear.
- Do not overstate controls or claim unimplemented items are implemented.
- Use precise terminology and separate facts from assumptions.

## Maintenance
Add new durable preferences with a date. Do not replace this profile from one isolated edit; record changes as dated additions and ask the user before resolving conflicting preferences.
